# Fl4Write design preflight

This receipt ties the title, the stone and the purpose together before the mark lands. It follows the `kyanite-product-design-preflight` skill. A choice this file can't back is marked as an assumption.

## 1. Purpose

FL4WRITE is a self-hosted, multi-forge code-review bot. It finds flaws, repairs them and gets stronger each round.

- Outcome for the user: Maintainers get grounded findings and fixes across GitHub and Forgejo, with a readiness score per repo.

## 2. Title, stone and purpose

| Layer | Content | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| Title | FL4WRITE: flaw-to-write, a reviewer that repairs | The product README |
| Stone | Fluorite (CaF2), raw and unpolished, violet with sea-green zones | The owner asked for a RAW, imperfect version |
| Product fit | Phantoms record each growth round; a healed fracture is a flaw the crystal repaired and grew past. Together they show a self-improving security agent. | Our metaphor, built from the owner's pick of r7 B (seam) mixed with C (phantom) |

## 3. Rejected metaphors

- Keystone and gate compositions (rejected).
- Black clamps or metal bands (the owner questioned them).
- Polished, symmetric gem fluorite.

## 4. Material reference

Fluorite: cubic and octahedral habit, perfect octahedral cleavage, glassy lustre, colour zoning from violet to green, phantom growth zones common.

The landed render is `branding/fl4write.png`: One raw, slightly lopsided fluorite octahedron with nested phantom growth layers (dark violet core to sea-green skin) and a diagonal healed fracture seam.

## 5. Family law and form

- The icon must read as an icon: one compact, bold silhouette that holds at 32 px and 16 px. No scene, pedestal, text, rays or glow.
- The real mineral shows in broad regions.
- Family palette: the crystal material itself, turquoise `#00FFE6` edges, magenta `#FF1A6E` and yellow `#FFD11A` catches, white speculars, dark inner planes, transparent background.
- Form: A frosted octahedron with stepped faces and chipped edges; the phantoms show through the faces and the seam crosses them.
- Approval: the owner's eye-gate (2026-11-10). A concept SVG is never the icon; the mark is a pipeline render.

## 6. Typography

- Wordmark: **FL4WRITE** in Chakra Petch Bold 700, uppercase, unmodified glyphs, native kerning.
- Endorsement: "by KyaniteLabs" in Chakra Petch Medium 500.
- The OFL licence ships with every export. On a dark canvas, invert only the ink.
